Works like a form field

Form-associated via ElementInternals: name, required, FormData and form reset just work.

One file, no build step

One readable ES module (about 44 kB gzipped) with a pinned Squire built in, typed with a .d.ts. Map it in an import map and go.

Images done properly

Paste, drop or pick images, upload them to your endpoint, then drag-resize, align and add alt text.

Your design system

Every color, font, size and radius is a --ve-* CSS variable. Light and dark mode included.

Your icons, your toolbar

Replace any icon with your own markup, or hide the toolbar and build your own with editor.exec().

Safe HTML

Pasted content is sanitized in the browser, and a matching Go (bluemonday) policy is ready for your server.
